# Uber Ride Analytics

## Overview

An end-to-end Uber ride analytics project focused on understanding ride-booking demand, customer behavior, operational performance, and business trends.

The project combines Python-based data analysis with business-focused visualization and Power BI-oriented analytics to turn raw ride-booking data into actionable insights.

## Business Objective

The objective is to analyze Uber ride-booking data to understand:

- When ride demand is highest
- Peak vs. off-peak booking patterns
- Cancellation and fulfillment behavior
- Factors affecting successful ride completion
- Operational opportunities to better align driver availability with customer demand

## Key Areas of Analysis

### Demand & Time Analysis
Understanding when customers book rides helps Uber align driver availability with demand, reduce unmet demand, and improve ride completion.

### Peak vs. Off-Peak Analysis
Comparing operational behavior during high- and low-demand periods helps identify opportunities for better resource allocation.

### Cancellation & Fulfillment Analysis
Analyzing cancellations and successful ride fulfillment helps identify operational gaps and areas that may affect customer experience.
