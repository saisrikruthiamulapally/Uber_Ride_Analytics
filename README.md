# 🚗 Uber Ride Analytics

### Turning ride-booking data into actionable business insights

An end-to-end **data analytics and business intelligence project** analyzing Uber ride-booking data to uncover demand patterns, operational performance, cancellation behavior, and opportunities to improve ride fulfillment.

**Python • Pandas • NumPy • Matplotlib • Seaborn • Jupyter • Power BI • Git/GitHub**

---

## 📌 Business Objective

Understanding when and why customers book rides helps Uber better align driver availability with demand, reduce unmet demand, and improve ride completion.

This project analyzes ride-booking data to answer key business questions around:

* 📈 **Demand patterns** — When are customers booking rides?
* ⏰ **Time-based trends** — How does demand change across hours and periods?
* 🚦 **Peak vs. off-peak operations** — How does operational performance differ during high- and low-demand periods?
* ❌ **Cancellations** — Where are cancellations occurring and what patterns can be identified?
* ✅ **Fulfillment** — How effectively are ride requests being completed?
* 💡 **Business opportunities** — What insights can support better operational decision-making?

## 📊 Key Analysis Areas

### 1. Demand & Time Analysis

Understanding when customers book rides helps identify demand patterns and provides a basis for better driver allocation and operational planning.

### 2. Peak vs. Off-Peak Operational Analysis

Comparing high-demand and low-demand periods helps identify changes in booking behavior and operational performance, supporting more efficient resource allocation.

### 3. Cancellation & Fulfillment Analysis

Analyzing cancellations and successful ride fulfillment helps identify operational gaps that may affect both customer experience and overall ride completion.
